About Fraiza Dollar

Fraiza Dollar is your go to online store for high quality cusotm apparel and trendy and designs.We speciaclize in bringing your creative ideas to life with personalized and stylish collections.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for textile and identity, Fraiza Dollar began as a small workshop dedicated to elevating everyday fabric into meaningful brand experiences.

What started with simple custom prints has grown into a full-service branding studio. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern design techniques to deliver cloth products that speak for your brand — loudly and clearly.

Today, we partner with startups, established businesses, and creative agencies who understand that clothing is more than fabric — it’s wearable storytelling.

Our Mission

To empower brands with high-quality, thoughtfully designed cloth products that carry their identity with clarity, durability, and style. Every stitch, print, and finish is intentional.
